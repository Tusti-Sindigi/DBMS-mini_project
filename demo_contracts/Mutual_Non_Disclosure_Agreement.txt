1. Purpose. The parties wish to explore a business opportunity and will exchange proprietary information under this Non-Disclosure Agreement.

2. Mutual Confidentiality. Each receiving party agrees to hold all confidential information in strict confidence and use the same standard of care it uses to protect its own sensitive business data of similar nature, but no less than reasonable care.

3. Standard Exclusions. Confidential information does not include information that is publicly known, already known to recipient before disclosure, or independently developed without reference to the disclosing party's data.

4. Term and Return. This agreement and the confidentiality obligations hereunder shall remain in effect for two (2) years from the effective date. Upon written request, all confidential materials will be promptly returned or destroyed.

5. Mutual Remedies. Both parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm and agree that either party may seek equitable injunctive relief in court.

6. Governing Law. This agreement is governed by the laws of California without regard to its conflict of law principles.
