1. Premises and Term. Landlord leases to Tenant commercial office Suite 400 for a period of twelve (12) calendar months starting on January 1, 2025.

2. Rent and Due Dates. Monthly rent of $3,500 is due on the first day of each calendar month. A grace period of five (5) business days is provided before standard administrative late fees apply.

3. Security Deposit. Tenant provides a security deposit of one month's rent. Landlord shall return the full deposit within thirty (30) days of move-out, less any documented repair costs for tenant-caused damages exceeding normal wear and tear.

4. Maintenance Responsibilities. Landlord maintains structural elements, roof, and central HVAC systems, while Tenant maintains interior cleanliness and minor fixtures.

5. Mutual Notice of Renewal. Either party may offer or decline lease extension by providing written notice sixty (60) days prior to lease end. The lease shall not automatically renew without express written mutual consent.

6. Dispute Resolution. The parties agree to attempt good-faith informal mediation prior to filing any formal legal claims.
